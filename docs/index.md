# LMS Ads Ecosystem

**Project type:** UX Design · Information Architecture · Content Strategy  
**Status:** In Planning  
**Last updated:** 2026-05-01

---

## What we're building

LinkedIn Ads content lives across a fragmented set of pages — product overviews, tips & best practices, and technical specs are each their own isolated destination. Users who arrive from search hit dead ends: no cross-links, no clear hierarchy, no path to related content.

This project redesigns the connective tissue between those pages — creating a cohesive, navigable ecosystem that makes it easy to move from specs to tips to product context, and back again.

---

## Goal

> Design a cohesive information architecture and navigation ecosystem for LinkedIn Ads pages that **eliminates search-landing dead ends** and measurably increases cross-page navigation between specs, tips, and product pages.

## Recommended Interlinking Approach

!!! success "Design Recommendation"
    **Baseline:** Concept D — Breadcrumbs + inline contextual links (lowest AEM lift; SEO benefit)  
    **Enhancement:** Concept B — Related content cards at page bottom (compatible with existing AEM card components)

    Together these cover above-content hierarchy discovery and below-content navigation, with minimal new engineering work.
