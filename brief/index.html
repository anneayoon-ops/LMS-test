    Brief & Goals¶

    Project context, problem statement, and technical foundation

    Problem Statement¶

    LinkedIn Ads content lives across a fragmented set of pages — product overviews, tips & best practices, and technical specs are each separate, siloed destinations. Users who arrive from search hit dead ends: no cross-links, no clear hierarchy, no path to related content.

    User pain point: Users entering via search encounter isolated Ads pages with no clear pathways to related content, creating dead ends that disrupt their ability to complete tasks efficiently.

    Goal¶

    Eliminate search-landing dead ends across LinkedIn Ads pages by creating a cohesive IA and navigation ecosystem that increases cross-page navigation between product, tips, and specs pages.

    Target Audience¶

    Primary: Digital marketers and advertisers who discover LinkedIn Ads content via organic search and need quick access to ad specs, best practices, and product information to plan and execute campaigns.

    Entry behavior: Search-first — users land deep in the content hierarchy (specs or tips pages) and need to discover broader context or adjacent resources.

    LLM optimization context

    The Ads Guide hub was recently updated for LLM/AI optimization. Design must support both human navigation and modular, self-contained content units that AI systems can parse. Each spec row and content section should be a discrete, complete unit — not dependent on surrounding context.

    Architecture¶

    Pipeline: Discovery → IA Map → Pattern Exploration → Wireframes → Design Spec → Hi-Fi → Content → Handoff → Localization

    AEM component feasibility is validated before every hi-fi commitment.

    Tech Stack¶

    Layer Tool
    CMS / Production AEM (Adobe Experience Manager) via LEM
    Hi-fi design Figma + Our Community Design System
    IA mapping FigJam
    Research baseline SEO analytics + competitive benchmarking

    Biggest risk: AEM component limitations

    Any interlinking or navigation pattern must be validated against what is buildable in AEM before hi-fi commitment. New components require engineering estimates and may impact timeline.

    KPI¶

    Cross-page navigation rate between Ads ecosystem content — measured via analytics on session depth and inter-page click events post-launch.

    Localization¶
